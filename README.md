# Hi there, I'm Sunil 👋

I am a **PhD Scholar and Teaching Assistant** specializing in **Fuzzy Differential Equations and Numerical Analysis**. My research focuses on developing rigorous mathematical models and numerical solvers to capture ambiguity and uncertainty in real-world dynamical systems.

---

### 🔬 Research Interests
* **Fuzzy Initial & Boundary Value Problems:** Investigating structural behavior, differentiability concepts (such as gH-differentiability), and solution geometry under epistemic uncertainty.
* **Numerical Methods:** Building and implementing robust algorithms (like Runge-Kutta methods and Crank-Nicolson schemes) adapted for interval-valued and fuzzy differential equations.
* **Fuzzy Dynamical Systems:** Applying mathematical frameworks to evaluate uncertainty, including fuzzy matrix games and computational optimization models.
